# Business model — BTN

Market-entry compliance for the Kingdom of Bhutan.

- Ministry of Finance (Procurement Management and Development Division,
  Department of Procurement and Properties) public procurement --
  e-GP (`egp.gov.bt`), a live self-service e-tendering portal, governed
  by the Procurement Rules and Regulations 2025 (see
  `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`)
- Corporate Regulatory Authority of Bhutan (CRA) company/business
  registration (Companies Act of Bhutan 2016, Chapter 14 Regulatory
  Authority)
- Department of Revenue and Customs (DRC) Taxpayer Number (TPN)
  registration via RAMIS (Income Tax Act of Bhutan 2025 ss.296/299)
- Foreign Direct Investment Rules and Regulations 2025 (Ministry of
  Industry, Commerce and Employment, Department of Industry) --
  Schedule III Negative List bars FDI in 9 named sectors (News Media,
  Distribution Services, standalone/primary-form mineral extraction,
  sub-crushing mineral processing, Hotel 3 Star and Below, General
  Health Services, non-compliant Certificate-of-Origin industries,
  Real Estate Businesses, and the Royal Government's own Prohibited
  List)
